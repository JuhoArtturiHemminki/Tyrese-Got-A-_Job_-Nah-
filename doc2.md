### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This engineering specification outlines the secondary energy harvesting network for the $76\text{-metric ton}$ Ekranoplan platform, **PROJECT FLAGCARRIER**, powered by a $157.6\text{ MW}$ Magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) Laser-Plasma core. **DOC-2** details a multi-stage mechanical regeneration layer—including a coaxial hydro-turbine and a Ventral Ram Air Turbine (RAT) network—that captures residual fluid and pneumatic momentum, feeding up to $36.80\text{ MW}$ back into the $12,500\text{ VDC}$ ultracapacitor grid.

---

## SECTION 1: HIGH-VELOCITY COAXIAL HYDRO-TURBINE (ZONE 4)

The exit profile of Zone 4 features a hubless, variable-pitch coaxial hydro-turbine utilizing magnetic levitation coupling and active pitch matrices to capture torque. Operating downstream of the MHD acceleration cell ($v_{\text{exit, MHD}} = 180\text{ m/s}$), it reduces exhaust velocity to $130\text{ m/s}$, yielding a net recovered mechanical power ($P_{\text{hydro, net}}$) of **$31.22\text{ MW}$** after accounting for system efficiencies and drag coefficients.

---

## SECTION 2: VENTRAL RAM AIR TURBINE (RAT) EXPANSION MATRIX

The Ventral RAT Matrix uses compressed air beneath the delta-wing ground-effect cushion via micro-venturi ducts. Internal supersonic expansion elevates local velocities to $v_{\text{local}} = 200\text{ m/s}$, producing a captured gross pneumatic power ($P_{\text{rat, gross}}$) of approximately **$0.56\text{ MW}$**.

---

## SECTION 3: RECONCILED CONVERGENT VOID & RECOVERY MATRICES

Conditioned via SiC MOSFET rectifiers and solid-state transformers, the recovered power feeds back into the central $12,500\text{ VDC}$ bus. This reduces net power demand from $157.60\text{ MW}$ to **$120.80\text{ MW}$** ($23.35\%$ reduction), extends mission operational profiles by $+30.47\%$, and mitigates thermal stress.

---

## SECTION 4: FLUID HOMOGENIZATION & WAKE STABILIZATION BENEFITS

The mechanical turbine array acts as an energy-extracting flow straightener by suppressing vortices, preventing cavitation erosion, and attenuating low-frequency acoustic noise.
